Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

SideFlip is operated by Tourbillion LLC, a Pennsylvania limited liability company. This policy explains how Tourbillion LLC collects, uses, shares, retains, and deletes information when you use the SideFlip website or iPhone app. We do not sell personal information or share it with advertisers.

Information we collect

  • Account and authentication information — your email address, SideFlip account identifier, authentication and session information, and preferences such as currency, language, onboarding status, and analytics choice.
  • Project content — information you choose to enter, including project titles, categories, status, purchase and sale information, expenses, notes, item identifiers such as model or serial numbers, VIN or hull number, photos, receipts, and Trade-Up Goal information such as targets, contributions, cash-outs, trades, and ledger entries.
  • Subscription and purchase records — purchase provider, product or plan, transaction and original-transaction identifiers, subscription status, and purchase and expiration dates. Apple processes payment credentials for iPhone purchases, and Stripe processes payment credentials for web purchases. SideFlip does not receive or store your complete card number or Apple ID password.
  • Usage analytics, when you enable them — pseudonymous app opens, screens, features, signup and onboarding steps, paywall interactions, purchase or restore outcomes, app version, and campaign or referral parameters.
  • Request and security information — hosting providers may process IP addresses, device or browser information, request metadata, and security logs when requests reach SideFlip.

Usage analytics may use a generated device or session identifier and your SideFlip account ID after sign-in. For verified subscription payments, analytics may include the provider, plan, status, amount, and currency. Analytics do not include project titles or descriptions, listing text, photos, project purchase or sale prices, expense amounts, card details, credentials, or signed purchase data. Session replay, cross-app advertising tracking, surveys, and automatic push-notification tracking are disabled.

How we use information

  • To authenticate your account and provide SideFlip features
  • To store and synchronize your projects, expenses, photos, receipts, and goals
  • To verify subscriptions, restore purchases, and provide Pro access
  • To send transactional emails such as account notices and receipts
  • To create a sales-listing draft from project details when you request that feature
  • To provide support, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, and improve the service

Service providers

We use service providers to operate SideFlip:

  • Supabase — authentication, database hosting, and file storage (privacy policy)
  • Apple — iPhone purchases, subscription verification, and subscription management (privacy policy)
  • Stripe — web subscription payments and billing management (privacy policy)
  • Vercel — website and server API hosting (privacy policy)
  • Resend — transactional email delivery (privacy policy)
  • Anthropic — creates a listing draft when you request that feature using the project details needed for the request (privacy policy)
  • PostHog — consent-based product analytics with session replay disabled (privacy policy)

We require service providers to process information only for the services they provide to SideFlip and to protect it consistently with this policy and applicable law. We share only the information needed for the requested service.

Photos

Photos you add to projects and receipts are stored in Supabase Storage. SideFlip currently serves these images through public, non-expiring URLs. The URLs are designed to be difficult to guess, but they are not access-controlled; anyone who has or obtains an image URL can view the image without signing in. We do not use your photos for advertising or sell them.

Retention and account deletion

We retain account and project information while your account remains active. You can initiate permanent account deletion in the iPhone app by opening Settings → Delete Account, or contact us for assistance. The deletion process removes your SideFlip authentication account, profile, projects, expenses, goals, ledger records, and files stored under your account.

Deleting your SideFlip account does not cancel a subscription. Apple subscriptions must be managed through Apple ID subscription settings; web subscriptions must be managed through SideFlip Settings or with support before account deletion. Apple, Stripe, and other providers may retain transaction, billing, fraud-prevention, security, or legal records under their own policies.

SideFlip retains a minimal deletion-suppression record containing account and subscription identifiers only as needed to prevent delayed Apple or Stripe events from recreating a deleted account and to meet security or legal obligations. If you enabled PostHog analytics, SideFlip submits a deletion request for the associated analytics identity and events; provider processing may be asynchronous.

Your choices and rights

You may disable product analytics at any time in SideFlip Settings. You may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information and ask questions about its use by contacting us below. Where applicable, you may withdraw consent or object to certain processing. We will verify and respond to requests as required by applicable law.

Children

SideFlip is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

SideFlip uses encrypted HTTPS connections, authenticated server endpoints, server-side subscription verification, access controls, and data-minimization measures. No service can guarantee absolute security; please use a unique password and contact us if you believe your account has been compromised.

Changes

If we make significant changes to this policy, we will post the updated policy and notify you by email or in the app when appropriate.
