Account and login help

Visit sideflip.org or open the SideFlip iPhone app to sign in. If you forgot your password, choose Forgot password? on the sign-in screen. If you cannot complete the reset, email support from the address associated with your account.

When contacting support, include the email address associated with your account and a brief description of the problem. Never send your password, authentication links, Apple purchase payloads, or complete payment-card information.

Subscriptions and billing

Compare the Free, Monthly Pro, and Annual Pro packages on the public Plans and Pricing page. SideFlip does not offer a free trial for web subscriptions. Management and refunds depend on where you subscribed.

Apple subscriptions

If you subscribed in the SideFlip iPhone app, Apple processes the purchase and charges your Apple ID. Manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple ID subscription settings. App Store charges and refund requests are handled by Apple. Deleting your SideFlip account does not cancel Apple billing.

Web subscriptions

  1. Sign in at sideflip.org.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. If a Stripe subscription is linked, select Manage Web Subscription.
  4. Use Stripe's secure billing portal to update payment information or cancel.

If no Stripe subscription is linked to the login, Settings shows No active web subscription and provides a View Pro Plans link instead of opening the billing portal. Existing web subscribers whose billing record needs to be recovered can select Already subscribed on web? Find Web Subscription.

If you cannot access your account, contact support before deleting it so we can help you manage any active web subscription.

Unexpected charge or billing problem

For an Apple charge, use Apple's purchase-history and refund-support tools. For a web charge, email SideFlip support with your account email, charge date, and amount. Do not include a full card number, password, or Apple ID credentials.

Delete your SideFlip account

In the iPhone app, sign in and open Settings → Delete Account. Review the notice, type DELETE, and confirm. This permanently removes your SideFlip account and core app content.

Account deletion does not cancel an Apple or Stripe subscription. Manage the subscription with Apple or Stripe first. If you cannot access the app, email support from the address associated with your account.

Technical and project-data help

For problems with projects, expenses, receipts, photos, analytics, generated listings, purchases, or Restore Purchases, tell us:

  • Whether you are using the SideFlip website or iPhone app
  • What you were trying to do
  • What happened instead, including any non-sensitive error message
  • Your device and browser or app version, if known

Do not email passwords, Social Security numbers, complete payment-card details, Apple ID credentials, signed purchase data, or private information visible on receipts.
