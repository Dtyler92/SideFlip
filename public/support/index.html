Account and login help

Visit sideflip.org to sign in. If you forgot your password, choose Forgot password? on the sign-in screen and follow the reset instructions sent to your email.

When contacting support, include the email address associated with your account and a brief description of the problem. Never send your password or complete payment-card information.

Subscription and billing

SideFlip offers a 7-day free trial followed by the plan selected during enrollment: $12.99 charged monthly or $99.99 charged annually (equivalent to $8.33 per month, billed annually). Subscriptions renew automatically until canceled.

Manage or cancel your subscription

  1. Sign in at sideflip.org.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Manage Subscription.
  4. Use Stripe's secure billing portal to update payment information or cancel.

Cancel before the trial ends to avoid the first charge. Canceling a paid subscription prevents its next renewal; access normally continues through the current paid period.

Unexpected charge or billing problem

Email support with your SideFlip account email, the charge date, and the amount. Do not include a full card number. Completed charges are non-refundable except where required by law, but we will review billing errors promptly.

Technical and project-data help

For problems with projects, expenses, receipts, photos, analytics, or generated listings, tell us:

  • Whether you are using the SideFlip website or iPhone app
  • What you were trying to do
  • What happened instead, including any error message
  • Your device and browser or app version, if known

Do not email passwords, Social Security numbers, complete payment-card details, or private information visible on receipts.
